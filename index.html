Keys

  

    
These work while the cube panel has focus, so typing in the moves box never turns the cube.

    

      
U D L R F B
Turn that face. Add Shift to turn it back.

      
M E S x y z
Slices and whole-cube rotations, the same way.

      
Space
Play or pause.

      
← →
Step back or forward.

      
[ ]
Previous or next chunk boundary.
